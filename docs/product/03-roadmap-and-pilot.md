# 3. Roadmap and pilot

## Now / Next / Later

| Horizon | What | Exit condition |
|---|---|---|
| **Now (v0.1, done)** | Screening rules, classification proposals, obligations by role, evidence checks, status, literacy coverage, executive pack (US-01 to US-11) | Pilot committee runs cycle 1 on the pack |
| **Next (v0.2, one sprint)** | Changes since the last review (US-12); plain-language screening questionnaire for owners (US-13) | Owners complete screening without the governance lead's help |
| **Later** | Annex I product route (US-14); rules refresh and re-run when the Commission's Art. 6 guidelines land (US-15); export to the GRC tool the organisation already uses | Counsel uses the pack as the starting point for every new classification |

## Pilot: two committee cycles

- **Who:** one executive or AI steering committee, its governance lead, one in-house counsel, the
  owners of 8-15 live or pilot use cases.
- **Week 0:** baseline the hours spent preparing the current AI Act status (H1); export the
  register, evidence and literacy data; owners answer the screening.
- **Cycle 1:** counsel reviews every proposal before the meeting and records agree / change, with
  the reason (H2). The committee meets on the pack.
- **Cycle 2:** re-run on updated evidence; count complete screenings (H3) and decisions citing the
  pack (H4).

## Decision rules, fixed before launch

- **Widen** to other committees or entities when preparation is under one hour **and** counsel
  changed or confirmed every classification without rewriting the reasons from scratch.
- **Iterate** when preparation time fell but counsel rewrote more than a third of the reasons:
  the screening questions are the problem, so US-13 comes first.
- **Stop** when neither preparation time nor committee decisions moved after two cycles.
